GD ARENA — WHAT TO SAY DURING YOUR PRESENTATION

My project is a Python-based group discussion practice application. It has ten
topics, a text and voice interface, and a trained stance-classification model.

The machine-learning task is: given an English argument and a target
proposition, predict whether it supports, opposes or is neutral toward that
proposition. I use TF-IDF features, Logistic Regression, and a Linear SVM
comparison. I selected the saved model on validation data, then evaluated
separately on held-out public and authored GD examples.

In the demo I select a topic and speak. After a 2-second pause, English speech
is transcribed and automatically submitted. I can turn auto share off to review
first, or type a point. Pause detection is browser audio logic, not trained ML.
The Python server loads the saved model and returns its prediction. Three
practice partners rotate through prepared perspectives selected by relevance.
The browser can read their replies aloud. I can pause, end the session, and
download a transcript and recap.

Hindi/Hinglish speech is transcribed by a pretrained Whisper model on the Python server. I review its English
meaning before sending it to the English-only classifier. I have not trained
a speech-recognition or translation model.

The public test accuracy is 43.31%; the authored GD test accuracy is 63.33%.
These results make this a baseline learning project, not a dependable
interview scoring system. Some predictions will be wrong. Future work is
more human-labelled GD data, better neutral detection, multilingual modelling,
and evaluating a genuinely generative discussion partner separately.

If asked about data: show DATA_SOURCES.txt, the training notebook and the
train/dev/test split columns. The 300 GD examples are assistant-authored
synthetic examples, not real candidate interview recordings.

If asked what YOU understand: explain the feature-to-classifier pipeline,
the reason for held-out testing, macro F1, and the difference between stance
and quality. Customize and study the code before presenting it as your work.
